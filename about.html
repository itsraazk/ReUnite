About ReUnite

OUR PURPOSE

Helping paws find their way home.

ReUnite connects dog owners and helpful finders through one simple idea: a permanent QR identity with a profile that can stay up to date.

🐾 Dog-first🔒 Privacy-aware📱 Mobile-friendly
THE IDEA

How ReUnite works

01

Register your dog

Add a name, breed, photo and useful identifying details.

02

Get a unique QR identity

Every dog receives a permanent identity such as REUNITE-UP-847291.

03

Keep information current

The QR itself does not need to change when the profile changes.

04

Mark your dog as lost

Add when and where they were last seen plus any helpful notes.

05

Connect through the profile

A finder can view the relevant information and, for a lost pet, send a private message.

BUILT AROUND TRUST

Helpful without oversharing

🔒 Owner privacy

Direct phone and email details are not the public QR experience.

🔄 Live status

Lost or safe status can change without replacing the QR identity.

📷 Easy scanning

A finder can use the QR scanner or enter the ReUnite identity manually.

💬 Lost-pet chat

When a pet is lost, communication can happen through the profile.

For owners 🐶

Register once, keep your pet's profile useful, and be ready before an emergency happens.

For finders 🤝

If you come across a ReUnite QR, you do not need to know the owner personally. Scan the identity, read the latest information and follow the available contact path.

Every scan is a small chance to turn a worried “Where are they?” into a happy “They're home.”

ReUnite — one identity, one connection, one more chance.
Our promise

Technology should help people care.

ReUnite is designed around one simple idea: when a pet gets separated from its family, the path back home should be simple, private and easy to understand.

🐶

Pet-first

Useful identity and details are kept around the pet, not buried in complicated steps.

🛡️

Privacy-aware

Public scanners can get the information they need without exposing private owner details.

❤️

Human-friendly

Clear language and simple actions matter when someone has just found a lost pet.
